About Novare

Professional Care. Personalized Treatment.

A modern outpatient behavioral health practice focused on thoughtful evaluation, evidence-based treatment, and collaborative care.

Your Mental Health Deserves Thoughtful Care

Novare Health Solutions LLC is an outpatient behavioral health practice serving individuals throughout Maryland.

Led by Oluchi Ukah, PMHNP-BC, Novare Health Solutions provides psychiatric assessment, diagnostic evaluation, medication management, and ongoing behavioral health care.

We believe effective psychiatric care begins with listening, careful assessment, and understanding each individual beyond a diagnosis. Our approach combines clinical expertise with individualized treatment planning and ongoing collaboration.

We offer convenient telehealth services and appropriate in-person appointments for eligible Maryland patients.

Our Approach

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation
  • Individualized treatment planning
  • Evidence-based medication management
  • Patient-centered care
  • Telehealth and in-person services
  • Focus on long-term wellness
Our Philosophy

Guided by Purpose and Clinical Excellence

To provide compassionate, accessible, and evidence-based outpatient behavioral healthcare that empowers individuals to improve mental health, restore stability, and pursue meaningful and fulfilling lives.

Our Vision

To build a trusted and respected behavioral health practice recognized for clinical excellence, patient-centered care, accessibility, and individualized treatment.
