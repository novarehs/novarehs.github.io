Meet Oluchi Ukah, MSN, PMHNP-BC

Hi, I’m Oluchi Ukah, a board-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner and the founder of Novare Health Solutions LLC. I hold an advanced nursing degree from Walden University, where I specialized in cutting-edge psychiatric care. Our Clinical Specialties

Psychiatric Evaluations

Every treatment pathway begins with a thorough, one-on-one initial diagnostic evaluation. Together, we will review your symptoms, history, and goals to build your baseline care map.

Medication Management

When indicated, psychopharmacology is a powerful tool. We provide careful, conservative prescribing, ongoing monitoring, and routine follow-ups to optimize your strategy while minimizing side effects.

Supportive Therapy

Medication is most effective when paired with emotional insight. Every follow-up integrates supportive psychotherapy and holistic coping frameworks to build lasting resilience.

Conditions We Safely Manage:

  • Depression and Persistent Mood Disturbances
  • Generalized Anxiety, Panic Attacks, and Social Phobias
  • Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)
  • Sleep-Wake Disruptions and Chronic Insomnia
  • Stress Management and Maladaptive Life Transitions
