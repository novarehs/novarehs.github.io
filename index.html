About Novare

Thoughtful care for the whole person.

Novare Health Solutions LLC is an outpatient behavioral health practice focused on providing individualized psychiatric care for adults throughout Maryland.

We recognize that every person's mental health journey is different. Our approach combines careful psychiatric assessment, evidence-based treatment, collaborative decision-making, and ongoing clinical monitoring.

Our goal is not simply to manage symptoms. We work with patients to develop practical treatment plans that support stability, functioning, and meaningful quality of life.

Your care should be individualized, understandable, collaborative, and grounded in evidence.

Our Approach

  • Comprehensive psychiatric assessment
  • Individualized treatment planning
  • Evidence-based medication management
  • Collaborative decision-making
  • Ongoing monitoring and follow-up
  • Respectful and patient-centered communication
