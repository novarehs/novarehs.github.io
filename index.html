About Novare

Professional Care. Personalized Treatment.

A modern outpatient behavioral health practice focused on thoughtful evaluation, evidence-based treatment, and collaborative care.

Novare Health Solutions LLC is an outpatient behavioral health practice serving individuals throughout Maryland. Led by Oluchi Ukah, PMHNP-BC, the practice provides psychiatric assessment, diagnostic evaluation, medication management, and ongoing behavioral health care.

We believe effective psychiatric care begins with listening, careful assessment, and understanding the individual beyond a diagnosis. Our approach combines clinical expertise with individualized treatment planning and ongoing collaboration with patients.

Services are designed to provide accessible and convenient care through secure telehealth and appropriate in-person appointments.

Our Approach

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluation
  • Individualized treatment planning
  • Evidence-based medication management
  • Collaborative patient care
  • Telehealth and in-person services
  • Focus on long-term wellness and stability
